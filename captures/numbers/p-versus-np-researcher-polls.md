# P versus NP researcher-poll results

William Gasarch's polls tracked belief about `P ≠ NP`. The measurements report opinion, not mathematical evidence.

## Measurements

| Poll year | Publication year | Respondents believing P ≠ NP | Total responses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2001 | 2002 | 61% | 100 |
| 2011 | 2012 | 83% | 151 |
| 2018 | 2019 | 88% | 124 |

Among experts in the 2018 poll, 99% believed `P ≠ NP`.

Third-poll source: https://www.cs.umd.edu/users/gasarch/BLOGPAPERS/pollpaper3.pdf

## Interpretation

The share believing `P ≠ NP` rose from 61% to 88% between the 2001 and 2018 polls. Gasarch cautioned that polling does not bring researchers closer to solving the problem or knowing when it will be solved; it reports subjective opinion for the period surveyed.
