# 2018 Ford F-150 5.0 Coyote V8 Engine Specifications

- **Output**: 395 hp / 400 lb-ft
- **Configuration**: Naturally aspirated 5.0L V8
- **Transmission**: 10R80 10-speed automatic

## Comparison with Similar Vehicles

| Vehicle                       | HP    | Torque        |
|-------------------------------|-------|---------------|
| 2018 F-150 5.0 V8             | 395   | 400 lb-ft     |
| Toyota Hilux 2.8 diesel       | ~201  | 310–369 lb-ft |
| 2018 Toyota RAV4              | 176   | 172 lb-ft     |
| 2019+ Toyota RAV4 (gas)       | 203   | 184 lb-ft     |

The F-150 5.0 wins in horsepower against both. The Hilux diesel can come close in
torque, but at much lower RPM.
