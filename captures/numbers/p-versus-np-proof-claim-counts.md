# P versus NP proof-claim counts, 1986–2016

Gerhard J. Woeginger's catalog records purported solutions, not accepted proofs.

## Inventory

| Claim type | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Proofs of P = NP | 61 |
| Proofs of P ≠ NP | 49 |
| Other results, such as undecidability | 6 |
| **Total** | **116** |

Period: 1986–2016.

Source page: https://wscor.win.tue.nl/woeginger/P-versus-NP.htm

## Interpretation

The counts show repeated attempts to claim a resolution. They do not measure the probability that either answer is true, and P versus NP remains unsolved.
