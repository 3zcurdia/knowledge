# Apple M-Series SoCs

The M series is a family of system on a chip (SoC) used in Mac computers from November 2020 and later, iPad Pro tablets from April 2021 and later, iPad Air tablets from March 2022 and later, and Apple Vision Pro.

## Key Chips

### Apple M1
The M1, Apple's first system on a chip designed for use in Macs, is manufactured using TSMC's 5 nm process. Announced on November 10, 2020, it was first used in the MacBook Air, Mac mini and 13-inch MacBook Pro. It has 4 performance cores and 4 efficiency cores (8 CPU total), up to 8 GPU cores, 16 billion transistors, and operates at up to 3.2 GHz.

### Apple M1 Pro
A more powerful version of the M1 with six to eight performance cores, two efficiency cores, 14 to 16 GPU cores, 16 Neural Engine cores, up to 32 GB unified RAM with up to 200 GB/s memory bandwidth. Apple claimed CPU performance about 70% faster than M1, GPU about double. Operates at up to 3.2 GHz.

### Apple M1 Max
A larger version of M1 Pro: eight performance cores, two efficiency cores, 24 to 32 GPU cores, 16 Neural Engine cores, up to 64 GB unified RAM with up to 400 GB/s memory bandwidth. Can deliver up to 30 streams of 4K or 7 streams of 8K ProRes video playback. Operates at up to 3.2 GHz.

### Apple M1 Ultra
Consists of two M1 Max dies connected by Apple's UltraFusion interconnect. It has 114 billion transistors, 16 performance cores, 4 efficiency cores, 48 to 64 GPU cores and 32 Neural Engine cores; configurable with up to 128 GB unified RAM of 800 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at up to 3.2 GHz.

### Apple M2
Announced June 6, 2022 at WWDC. Made with TSMC's N5P process, contains 20 billion transistors (25% increase from M1). 8 CPU cores (4 performance, 4 efficiency), up to 10 GPU cores, up to 24 GB RAM, 100 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at up to 3.5 GHz. Apple claimed CPU improvements up to 18% and GPU up to 35% vs M1.

### Apple M2 Pro
Six to eight performance cores, four efficiency cores, 16 to 19 GPU cores, up to 32 GB RAM with 200 GB/s memory bandwidth. Apple claimed CPU 20% faster than M1 Pro, GPU 30% faster. Operates at up to 3.5 GHz.

### Apple M2 Max
Eight performance cores, four efficiency cores, 30 to 38 GPU cores, up to 96 GB RAM with 400 GB/s memory bandwidth. Apple claimed CPU 20% faster than M1 Max, GPU 30% faster. Operates at up to 3.6 GHz.

### Apple M2 Ultra
Two M2 Max dies via UltraFusion. 134 billion transistors, 16 performance cores, 8 efficiency cores, 60 to 76 GPU cores, up to 192 GB RAM of 800 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at up to 3.6 GHz.

### Apple M3
Announced October 30, 2023. Based on 3nm process (TSMC N3B), contains 25 billion transistors. 8 CPU cores (4 performance, 4 efficiency), up to 10 GPU cores. Operates at up to 4.1 GHz. Introduced hardware-accelerated ray tracing and mesh shading for Mac. Apple claimed CPU up to 35% and GPU up to 65% improvements vs M1.

### Apple M3 Pro
Five or six performance cores, six efficiency cores, 14 to 18 GPU cores, up to 36 GB RAM with 150 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at up to 4.1 GHz. Apple claimed CPU 30% faster than M1 Pro, GPU 40% faster.

### Apple M3 Max
Ten or twelve performance cores, four efficiency cores, 30 to 40 GPU cores, up to 128 GB RAM with 400 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at up to 4.1 GHz. Apple claimed CPU 80% faster than M1 Max, GPU 50% faster.

### Apple M3 Ultra
Two M3 Max dies via UltraFusion. 184 billion transistors, 20 or 24 performance cores, 8 efficiency cores, 60 to 80 GPU cores, up to 512 GB RAM of 800 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at up to 4.0 GHz.

### Apple M4
Announced May 7, 2024. Based on TSMC's N3E process. Up to 10 CPU cores (3 or 4 performance, 4 to 6 efficiency), up to 10 GPU cores. Operates at 4.4 GHz. First Apple silicon chip to use ARMv9.2-A instruction set. Apple claimed up to 1.5x faster CPU vs M2.

### Apple M4 Pro
Eight or ten performance cores, four efficiency cores, 16 to 20 GPU cores, up to 64 GB RAM with 273 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at 4.5 GHz. Apple claimed CPU 1.9x faster than M1 Pro, GPU 2x faster.

### Apple M4 Max
Ten or twelve performance cores, four efficiency cores, 32 to 40 GPU cores, up to 128 GB RAM with up to 546 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at 4.5 GHz. Apple claimed CPU 2.2x faster than M1 Max, GPU 1.9x faster.

### Apple M5
Announced October 15, 2025. Based on TSMC's N3P process. Up to 4 "super" cores, up to 6 performance cores, and up to 10 GPU cores. Clock frequency of 4.61 GHz on 14-inch MacBook Pro, 4.4 GHz on iPad Pro.

### Apple M5 Pro
Up to 6 "super" cores, up to 12 performance cores, up to 20 GPU cores, and 16 Neural Engine cores. Supports up to 64 GB RAM with 307 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at about 4.61 GHz. Apple claimed CPU 2.5x faster than M1 Pro, GPU 2.2x faster.

### Apple M5 Max
6 "super" cores, 12 performance cores, up to 40 GPU cores, and 16 Neural Engine cores. Supports up to 128 GB RAM with up to 614 GB/s memory bandwidth. Operates at about 4.61 GHz. Apple claimed CPU 2.5x faster than M1 Max, GPU 2.2x faster.
