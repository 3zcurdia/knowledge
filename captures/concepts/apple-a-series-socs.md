# Apple A-Series SoCs

The A series is a family of SoCs used in the iPhone, certain iPad models (including iPad Mini and entry-level iPad), MacBook Neo, and the Apple TV. A-series chips were also used in the discontinued iPod Touch line and the original HomePod. They integrate one or more ARM-based processing cores (CPU), a graphics processing unit (GPU), cache memory and other electronics necessary to provide mobile computing functions within a single physical package.

## Key Chips

### Apple A4
The Apple A4 is a PoP SoC manufactured by Samsung, the first SoC Apple designed in-house. It combines an ARM Cortex-A8 CPU and a PowerVR SGX 535 graphics processor (GPU), all built on Samsung's 45-nanometer silicon chip fabrication process. The design emphasizes power efficiency. The A4 commercially debuted in 2010 in the iPad, and was later used in the iPhone 4, fourth-generation iPod Touch, and the 2nd-generation Apple TV. It runs at 1 GHz in the first iPads and 2nd-generation Apple TV, and 800 MHz in the iPhone 4 and fourth-generation iPod Touch.

### Apple A5
The Apple A5 is an SoC manufactured by Samsung that replaced the A4. It debuted with the iPad 2 in March 2011. The A5 contains a dual-core ARM Cortex-A9 CPU with NEON SIMD and a dual-core PowerVR SGX543MP2 GPU. The CPU "can do twice the work" and the GPU has "up to nine times the graphics performance" compared to the A4, according to Apple. An updated 32 nm version provided around 15% better battery life during web browsing.

### Apple A6
The Apple A6 introduced Apple's custom Swift CPU core (ARMv7s-based), replacing licensed ARM cores. It is 22% smaller and draws less power than the 45nm A5. Manufactured by Samsung on a 32nm HKMG process with a 1.3 GHz dual-core CPU and triple-core PowerVR SGX 543MP3 GPU.

### Apple A7
The Apple A7 is the first 64-bit chip used in a smartphone (iPhone 5S, September 2013). It features an Apple-designed 1.3–1.4 GHz 64-bit ARMv8-A dual-core CPU called Cyclone, with 31 general-purpose 64-bit registers and 32 floating-point/NEON 128-bit registers. Manufactured by Samsung on 28nm HKMG with over 1 billion transistors on a 102 mm² die. Includes a dedicated Secure Enclave Processor for Touch ID.

### Apple A8
The Apple A8 is a 64-bit PoP SoC manufactured by TSMC (first A-series chip from TSMC), debuting in iPhone 6 and 6 Plus. Features a 1.4 GHz dual-core CPU and custom PowerVR GX6450 GPU. Manufactured on 20nm with 2 billion transistors. Apple claimed 25% more CPU performance and 50% more graphics performance while drawing only 50% of the power compared to A7.

### Apple A10 Fusion
The Apple A10 Fusion introduced ARM big.LITTLE quad-core design with two high-performance cores and two highly efficient cores. It is 40% faster than the A9, with 50% faster graphics, running at 2.34 GHz. Manufactured by TSMC on 16nm FinFET.

### Apple A11 Bionic
The Apple A11 Bionic features two high-performance cores (25% faster than A10), four high-efficiency cores (70% faster than A10's efficient cores), and Apple's first in-house three-core GPU. It is the first A-series chip to feature Apple's "Neural Engine" for AI/ML processes.

### Apple A12 Bionic
The Apple A12 Bionic is manufactured by TSMC using a 7nm FinFET process — the first 7nm chip to ship in a smartphone. It has two high-performance cores (15% faster than A11) and four high-efficiency cores (50% lower power than A11).

### Apple A14 Bionic
The Apple A14 Bionic is the first commercially available 5nm chipset, containing 11.8 billion transistors and a 16-core Neural Engine. It includes a 6-core CPU and 4-core GPU with real-time machine learning capabilities.

### Apple A15 Bionic
The Apple A15 Bionic is built on a 5nm process with 15 billion transistors. It has 2 high-performance cores, 4 high-efficiency cores, up to 5-core GPU, and a 16-core Neural Engine capable of 15.8 trillion operations per second.

### Apple A16 Bionic
The Apple A16 Bionic has 16 billion transistors on TSMC's N4P fabrication process (de facto 4th-gen 5nm). Memory upgraded to LPDDR5 for 50% higher bandwidth. Neural Engine: 17 trillion operations per second.

### Apple A17 Pro
The Apple A17 Pro is Apple's first 3nm SoC. It features a 6-core GPU described as the biggest redesign in Apple GPU history, adding hardware-accelerated ray tracing and mesh shading. Neural Engine: 35 trillion operations per second.

### Apple A18 and A18 Pro
Built on TSMC's N3E process with 2 high-performance and 4 high-efficiency cores. Use LPDDR5X for 17% higher memory bandwidth. The A18 Pro is also used in the MacBook Neo.

### Apple A19 and A19 Pro
Built on TSMC's N3P process. The A19 has 5-core graphics; A19 Pro has 6-core graphics. Also used in the Studio Display (2026) and Studio Display XDR.
