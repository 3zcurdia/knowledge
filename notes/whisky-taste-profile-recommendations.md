# Whisky Taste Profile and Recommendations

Personal whisky taste profile analysis based on ratings of Monkey Shoulder (10/10), Highland Park (8/10), Bulleit Bourbon (7/10), Buchanan's (5/10), and Jameson (3/10).

The profile indicates a preference for rich, malty, honeyed whiskies with light peat and coastal notes, avoiding thin grain-forward blends. Monkey Shoulder is a "vatted malt" (a blend of single malts including Balvenie and Glenfiddich), explaining deep vanilla and orange notes. Highland Park confirms appreciation for balanced peat and brine with honey and floral notes, rather than medicinal Islay smoke. Bulleit Bourbon shows enjoyment of spicy rye and heavy American oak char.

## Recommendations

- **Talisker 10 Year Old** — Isle of Skye malt with intense coastal character, cracked black pepper, dried fruits, and bonfire-on-the-beach smoke. Bridges sweetness of Monkey Shoulder with rugged coastal scotch.
- **Old Forester 1910** — Double-barreled bourbon with deep dark chocolate and roasted marshmallow sweetness. Richer and more sophisticated than Bulleit, with heavy oak influence.
- **Bunnahabhain 12 Year Old** — Unpeated Islay ("the Isis of Islay"), rich and oily, aged in Sherry casks. Provides deep nuttiness and dried fruit with a hint of sea salt.
