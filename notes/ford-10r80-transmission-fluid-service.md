# Ford 10R80 10-Speed Automatic Transmission Fluid Service

The 10R80 transmission (paired with 5.0 V8 in 2018+ F-150) is often labeled
"lifetime fluid" but in practice should be serviced regularly.

## Recommended Interval

- Every 30,000–60,000 miles (sooner if towing, hills, or hard use)

## Fluid Specification

**MERCON ULV (Ultra Low Viscosity)** only.

## Service Methods

| Method       | Fluid Replaced | Risk                        |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Drain & Fill | ~40–60%       | Safer, recommended          |
| Full flush  | ~100%         | Risky if fluid is old/dirty |

## Critical Details

- Fluid level must be checked at operating temperature (~190°F)
- Vehicle must be level when filling
- Overfilling or underfilling causes shifting problems
- DIY drain & fill: ~$80–150 (6–8 quarts at $10–15/quart)
- Shop service: $200–400

## Signs Service is Needed

- Rough shifting
- Delayed engagement
- Unknown service history
- Over ~60,000 miles
